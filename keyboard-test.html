Matrix & Switch Mechanics

Full 104-Key Layout & NKRO Rollover Explained

Learn how full Numpad keyboards process multi-key matrix scanning and prevent ghosting.

⚡

What is True NKRO?

Full N-Key Rollover means every single switch has its own dedicated SMD diode, allowing every key to register independently without blocking limits.

🔢

104-Key Numpad Architecture

Dedicated Numpads send distinct scan codes (Numpad0 vs Digit0). This distinction is critical for numeric data entry, CAD shortcuts, and blender camera views.

👻

Ghosting Detection Test

Hold both Shift keys and type: THE QUICK BROWN FOX JUMPS OVER THE LAZY DOG. If any letter fails, your matrix is jamming on multi-key combinations.

Frequently Asked Questions

A full 104-key ANSI keyboard includes the dedicated 17-key numeric keypad on the right. An 87-key Tenkeyless (TKL) keyboard removes the Numpad for a more compact footprint and increased desk space for mouse movement.

Certain OS/Browser hotkeys (like Ctrl+W, Ctrl+N, F5) have protected native browser actions. Our script calls event.preventDefault() on standard function keys, but browser security may retain focus on system shortcuts.
