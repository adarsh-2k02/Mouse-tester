2. Purpose & Scope of Services

MouseTester.io provides free, open, client-side web utilities intended for informational, diagnostic, educational, and hardware benchmarking purposes. These include:

  • Gamepad thumbstick drift, circularity error calculation, and trigger pressure telemetry.
  • Display inspection utilities (RGB dead pixel detection and monitor motion blur ghosting checkers).
