Camera Optimization

How to Improve Your Webcam Video Quality

Fix blurry camera output, low frame rates in low light, and auto-exposure flickering.

💡

Lighting & Exposure

Webcam sensors are small and struggle in dim lighting. Place a warm soft key light in front of your face to eliminate digital sensor noise.

🔌

USB 3.0 Bandwidth

High resolution 1080p60 and 4K webcams require dedicated USB 3.0/3.1 ports. Avoid sharing USB hubs with external hard drives.

🎯

Auto-Focus Hunting

If your webcam constantly focuses back and forth, disable continuous autofocus in your camera software and set manual focal distance.
